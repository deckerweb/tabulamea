TabulaMea
Privacy-friendly website statistics

TabulaMea brings clarity to your website statistics. How often are your pages viewed? Which posts attract attention? Which websites link people to your content? Get the numbers that help with everyday decisions, right inside WordPress, without cookies, visitor profiles or identifying returning people.

The name comes from the Latin “tabula mea”: “my board”. For this plugin, it also expresses “my overview” — a place of your own where a few measurements form a clear picture of your website. Use it privately to maintain and plan your content, or share selected results through rankings and pageview counters.

TabulaMea collects a small set of measurements and presents the recorded numbers in useful ways: yearly roundups, content rankings and pageview counts in your post and page lists. You decide who can see statistics and which results become public. The data stays in your WordPress installation.

Your content. Its impact. All in view.
